The Moment

Foundations Are Cheaper Than Repairs.

Most consultants assemble their private-practice presence piecemeal — a directory listing here, a rushed website there. Two years later, nothing connects and nothing measures. Starting correctly means each piece is built to work with the next.

The cost of fixing it later

  • Rebuilding a fragmented website

    A rushed site eventually has to be torn down and rebuilt around patients.

  • Correcting inconsistent profiles

    Mismatched listings take months of tedious work to align after the fact.

  • Reclaiming your name search

    The wrong results get harder to displace the longer they sit unchallenged.

  • Retrofitting measurement

    Without a baseline, nobody can say what changed, when, or why.

The WardShift First 90 Days

A Correct Order of Operations.

An implementation framework — not a promise of results. Pacing varies by practice.

Start here

Phase 1

Foundation

Weeks 1–4 — indicative pacing

  • Professional positioning
  • Domain & digital identity
  • Website — built around patients, not a CV
  • Professional profiles
  • Practice locations & listings
  • Analytics installed
  • Enquiry system designed

Outcome: a coherent, measurable presence exists.

Phase 2

Visibility

Weeks 5–9 — indicative pacing

  • Search foundations — technical + architecture
  • Important service pages
  • Local presence where eligible
  • Initial condition / procedure content
  • Reputation process begun

Outcome: the right searches start finding accurate answers.

Phase 3

Growth

Weeks 10–13 — indicative pacing

  • Organic expansion
  • Paid search where appropriate
  • Conversion improvements
  • Measurement review against baseline
  • Next-stage planning

Outcome: growth becomes a managed system, not a hope.

What You Avoid

The Three Expensive Mistakes of a Piecemeal Start.

A website that's a digital CV

Instead: a website built around patients — what they need, what they ask, how they decide.

Profiles that contradict each other

Instead: one accurate story, consistent everywhere your name appears.

Growth you can't measure

Instead: measurement installed from day one, so every step is visible.

Working With WardShift

How an Engagement Runs.

Most newly appointed consultants begin with a Growth Review — a structured look at objectives, timeline and anything that already exists. From there, the First 90 Days runs as a managed implementation in the correct order.

  1. Review. Where you are, where the practice needs to be, what exists already.
  2. Foundation build. Positioning, website, profiles, listings and measurement — installed to work together.
  3. Visibility. Search foundations, service pages and the beginnings of your professional reputation.
  4. Managed growth. Expansion measured against the baseline, sequenced against the practice's objectives.

WardShift handles the growth side, in the right order — you handle the clinical one.
