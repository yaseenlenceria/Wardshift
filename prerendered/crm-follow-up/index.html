What the practice sees

One Place for Every Enquiry.

The operational backbone of a growing practice: visibility, reminders and measurement — built for administrative work, not clinical decisions.

Central enquiry tracking

Every enquiry in one place — forms, calls and messages — with nothing lost between systems.

Enquiry source attribution

Each enquiry carries its source, so you know which channel produced it.

Administrative notes

Structured notes keep context with the enquiry, not in someone's memory.

Practice notifications

The right person is notified promptly when an enquiry needs attention.

Follow-up reminders

Reminders surface enquiries that are waiting, before they go quiet.

Appointment-request stages

Clear stages from new enquiry to booked appointment — visible at a glance.

Appropriate SMS / email

Administrative messages only — confirmations, logistics and instructions.

Reporting & trends

Enquiry volume, response and stage trends, reported plainly.

Conversion measurement

See how many enquiries become booked appointments — and where others stop.

Measurement

From Enquiry Source to Booked Outcome.

When source, stage and outcome live in one system, growth decisions become evidence: which channels produce appropriate enquiries, what they cost, and where the journey loses people.
