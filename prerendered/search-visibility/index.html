Search Behaviour

Six Ways Patients Search.

Visibility is a system, not a ranking trick. Each kind of search needs its own foundations.

01

Doctor-name searches

After a referral or recommendation, patients search your name. What appears is your first consultation room.

02

Specialty searches

Patients who know the specialty but not the doctor.

03

Condition searches

Patients searching symptoms and conditions they want investigated.

04

Procedure searches

High-intent searches from patients who know what they need.

05

Location searches

Proximity-driven discovery through local search and maps.

06

Referral-validation searches

The quiet majority of private-practice searches.

The Work

Visibility Is Engineered, Not Wished For.

Durable visibility is built in layers — each one depending on the layer beneath it.

Layer 3

Authority & presence

Professional authority, local search presence, and Google profiles where the practice is eligible.

Layer 2

Content & intent

Service pages, condition and procedure content where appropriate — planned around what patients actually search for.

Layer 1

Technical foundations

The foundation: a fast, well-structured website that Google can properly understand and rank.

We measure positions and trends honestly — and never promise specific rankings.

Measurement

Measured, Not Assumed.

We track search visibility, relevant visitors, and what those visitors do — calls, forms and enquiries by source — so visibility work is accountable to outcomes, not vanity metrics.
